Work Completed

After the first week, our naive implementation was fully working and the boilerplate of openGL-CUDA was fully in place. All the basic math for density, pressure, and wall collisions were also finished. In the second week, we worked on a serial CPU baseline against which we could measure speedups. We also worked on the nearest-neighbor search using a uniform grid and thrust's exclusive scan, similar to the circle renderer from assignment 2. Lastly, the CPU baseline was also used to verify correctness of our CUDA implementation. We currently have the particle simulation running with 64k particles at around 2.47ms per step.
