SPH Fluid Simulation in CUDA

Sami Sharif (ssharif) & Nathan Mehaffy (nheaffy)

Summary

We are going to implement a 3D fluid simulator based on Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) entirely in CUDA on the NVIDIA RTX 2080 GPUs in the GHC machines. Our goal is to scale the simulation to as many particles as possible, and to measure and explain the bottlenecks in neighbor search, memory access, and rendering along the way. If time allows, we will use OpenGL to render a continous surface with raymarching.
